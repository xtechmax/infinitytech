Shipping & Delivery Policy

Service turnaround & digital asset delivery terms

Thank you for choosing Infinity Tech. This Shipping and Delivery Policy covers both our digital marketing & software deliverables and any physical marketing merchandise.

1. Digital Products and Service Delivery

For all digital solutions (Website development, SEO audits, Branding assets, Graphic design, PPC campaigns, and analytics dashboards):

  • Deliverables are transferred electronically via secure cloud link, email, or GitHub repository within agreed project milestones.
  • Access credentials and asset files are shared promptly upon sign-off.

2. Physical Merchandise / Deliverables

If physical brand collateral or hardware items are ordered:

  • Orders are processed within 2–4 business days.
  • Standard shipping within India typically arrives within 5–7 business days depending on destination pincode.
  • Tracking information is shared via email once dispatched.

3. Delivery Inquiries & Delays

If you experience any delay in receiving your digital assets or shipment, please contact our support team immediately.
