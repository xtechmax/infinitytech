Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

At Infinity Tech, accessible from our digital platforms, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Infinity Tech and how we use it.

Information We Collect

The personal information that you are asked to provide, and the reasons why you are asked to provide it, will be made clear to you at the point we ask you to provide your personal information:

  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number, and company name when you request a consultation or quote.
  • Project specifications, requirements, and communication history.
  • Log and usage data regarding your interactions with our website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our digital services and website.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our offerings and client experience.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our website and services.
  • Develop new products, services, features, and functionality.
  • Communicate with you, either directly or through our customer care team, including for customer service, updates, and marketing purposes.
  • Send you emails, quotes, and project deliverables.
  • Find and prevent fraud and maintain network security.

Cookies and Tracking

Like any other website, Infinity Tech uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

Data Protection Rights

We want to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to request access, rectification, erasure, or restriction of their personal data.
