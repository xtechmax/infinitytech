About us

Personal guidance, made for you

PalmQ IND creates personalised digital reports using the birth details, preferences and palm information a customer chooses to provide.

Our reports cover areas such as life timing, relationships, career, money, name numerology and city compatibility. Each report is generated for the individual customer and delivered digitally. We do not ship a physical product.

Our purpose

We aim to make traditional astrology, numerology and palm-reading ideas easier to understand and useful for personal reflection. Our reports offer guidance and entertainment; they are not a substitute for medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

Location data

Birthplace search uses place data from GeoNames and, when needed, OpenStreetMap contributors.
