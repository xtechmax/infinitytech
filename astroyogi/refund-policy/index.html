Refund & cancellation policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Astro Yogi sells personalised digital astrology, numerology and palm-based reports. This policy explains when an order can be cancelled and when a refund is issued. It applies alongside our terms and conditions.

1. What you are buying

  • Every report is a digital product, generated for the individual customer from the details submitted at checkout.
  • Each purchase is a single one-time payment. Astro Yogi does not operate a subscription, auto-renewal or recurring-billing plan, so there is no recurring payment to cancel.
  • Nothing is physically shipped. See our delivery policy for how reports reach you.

2. Cancellations

Because there is no subscription or recurring charge, a cancellation only concerns the individual order you placed.

  • Before payment is completed. You may abandon or close the checkout at any point. No charge is raised and nothing needs to be cancelled.
  • After payment, before the report is generated. Write to us at [email protected]. If your report has not yet been generated and delivered, we will cancel the order and refund the full amount charged.
  • After the report has been generated and made available. The personalised report has been produced and delivered at that point, so the order can no longer be cancelled. A refund may still apply under section 3 below.
  • Failed or declined payments. These are not completed purchases and require no cancellation. If an amount was debited despite the failure, section 3 applies.

3. When we refund

We will refund the report charge in each of the following situations:

  • You were charged more than once for the same order.
  • Payment succeeded but the report was not delivered because of a technical issue on our side, and we are unable to restore access or resend it.
  • An amount was debited but the order never completed at the payment provider.
  • A paid Mahakundli does not show the birth details you submitted, the available calculated dasha dates, chart-specific placements or the evidence used for its chapters. We will first audit and correct or regenerate the report; if we cannot deliver the stated personalised report, we refund the report charge.
  • You cancelled a paid order before the report was generated, as described in section 2.

4. When we do not refund

  • Change of mind after a personalised report has already been generated and accessed.
  • Dissatisfaction with the guidance, predictions or interpretation, where the report was delivered as described. Reports are for personal guidance, reflection and entertainment, and they do not guarantee any particular outcome.
  • Incorrect birth date, birth time, birth place, name or answers supplied by the customer. Write to us and we will regenerate the report with corrected details wherever it is technically possible.

5. How to raise a refund or cancellation request

Email [email protected] within 7 days of the charge, from the email address used for the order, including:

  • The payment reference or order ID.
  • The date and amount of the charge.
  • A short description of the problem.

We acknowledge refund and cancellation requests within 2 working days and verify the payment with our payment provider before deciding.

6. Refund method and timeline

  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method only. We cannot refund to a different card, account or UPI ID.
  • We initiate an approved refund within 3 working days of approving it.
  • Once initiated, the amount typically reaches your account within 5 to 7 working days. The final credit timing is controlled by your bank, card network or UPI provider, not by Astro Yogi.
  • Refunds are made in the currency of the original charge for the amount actually received, inclusive of applicable taxes.

7. Grievances

If you are not satisfied with the outcome of a refund or cancellation request, reply on the same email thread and ask for it to be escalated. We will review the decision and respond with a final position.
