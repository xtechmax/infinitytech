Privacy policy

Your information and privacy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

1. Information we collect

Depending on the report you choose, we may collect:

  • Your name, birth date, birth time, birth place and report answers.
  • Your email address or phone number when you provide it for delivery or support.
  • Payment reference, amount and payment status. We do not store full card or UPI credentials.
  • For international checkout, your current residential address, country-of-residence declaration, edge-detected country and payment-country evidence supplied by the payment provider.
  • Basic device, browser, referral, analytics and security information.
  • A palm photo when you choose to submit one for a palm report.

2. How we use information

We use this information to create and deliver reports, process and reconcile payments, verify whether an international transaction is eligible for the offered checkout, maintain tax and remittance evidence, provide support, protect the service from misuse, improve the customer experience, measure and attribute advertising, and create or select advertising audiences where permitted by applicable law.

3. Palm photos

A palm photo is sent securely for line detection when you choose to use the palm feature. Astro Vela keeps the detected line points and report results, not the original palm photo.

4. Service providers and advertising platforms

We disclose information only as necessary to providers and platforms supporting payments, hosting, analytics, communications and advertising. Payment providers may include Razorpay, Cashfree and PayGlocal, depending on the storefront and transaction. For an international payment, PayGlocal may receive the transaction amount and currency, email address, residential billing details and technical risk data needed to process and reconcile the payment. We use Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates ("Meta"), including the Meta Pixel, Conversions API and Customer List Custom Audiences. Meta may receive limited device, browser, referral and transaction-event data for advertising delivery, measurement, attribution and audience selection. Email addresses or phone numbers used for customer-list matching are normalised and cryptographically hashed before transmission. We also use Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tracking. Google may receive limited device, browser, page, referral and transaction-event data for analytics, advertising measurement and attribution. We do not provide Meta or Google with palm photographs, report answers, birth information, report content, residential addresses or full payment credentials for advertising purposes. We do not sell personal information.

5. Storage and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain transaction, tax and remittance records, meet legal obligations and prevent misuse. Some payment and export records must be kept even if other profile information is deleted. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, although no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion tracking and the Meta Pixel, for session continuity, security, analytics, advertising measurement and audience selection. We disable Google advertising-personalisation signals on this service. Advertising preferences may be managed through Meta's ad settings, Google's My Ad Center and applicable browser or device controls.

7. Your choices

You may request correction or deletion of personal information associated with your report, subject to records retained for payment, security or legal purposes. You may also object to the use of your email address or phone number for Meta customer-list advertising by contacting us. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children

The international Palm experience is available only to adults aged 18 to 120. It checks date of birth before it accepts a palm photo. Our other report experiences are also intended for adults; do not submit personal information or an image about a minor.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy when our service or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
