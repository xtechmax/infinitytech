Shipping & delivery policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

1. Digital delivery only

PalmQ IND sells personalised digital reports. No physical product is shipped and no courier or tracking number is involved. We do not charge any shipping, handling or delivery fee. The checkout shows the amount charged by PalmQ IND; other taxes, card-conversion costs or bank charges may apply.

2. How your report reaches you

  • Your report is made available online in your browser immediately after the payment is confirmed, normally within a few minutes.
  • Where shown for that product, a PDF or email copy is also sent to the email address you provided at checkout.
  • Delivery depends on successful payment confirmation and on the accuracy of the contact details you supply.

3. Delivery timeline

  • Reports are generated automatically and are normally available immediately after payment confirmation.
  • If a payment provider takes longer to confirm a payment, delivery follows as soon as that confirmation reaches us.
  • If a report has not appeared within 24 hours of a successful payment, contact us and we will restore access or resend it.

4. If your report does not arrive

First check the spam or promotions folder of the email address used at checkout. If it is still missing, email [email protected] with your payment reference. After verifying the payment we will restore access or resend the report. If we cannot deliver it, our refund and cancellation policy applies.

5. Service area

Because delivery is entirely digital, there are no physical shipping restrictions or delivery charges. Purchase availability can still vary by storefront, country, payment-provider coverage and the location checks described at checkout.
