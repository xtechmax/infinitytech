Astroyogi is an online service providing personalised digital astrology, numerology and palm-based reports. Support is handled over email so that every request stays on a written record you can refer back to.

Email us

Write to [email protected] for any of the following:

  • A report you paid for has not arrived, or access to it was lost.
  • A payment question, a duplicate charge, or a refund or cancellation request.
  • A correction to the birth details, name or answers used in your report.
  • A privacy request, such as correction or deletion of your information.

Response times

  • We acknowledge emails within 2 working days.
  • Payment, refund and cancellation requests are verified with our payment provider before we respond with a decision.
  • Please write from the email address used for the order and include your payment reference, so we can locate the order quickly.
